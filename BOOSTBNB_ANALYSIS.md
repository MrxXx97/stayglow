# Boostbnb Platform Analysis

## 📋 Executive Summary

**Boostbnb** is an AI-powered photo enhancement platform specifically designed for Airbnb hosts and real estate professionals. The platform uses artificial intelligence to automatically enhance property photos, promising to increase booking rates and rental values.

**Website:** https://boostsbnb.com/

## ✨ AI-Powered Features

The platform offers several AI editing capabilities:

1. **Sky Replacement** - Replace dull skies with beautiful alternatives
2. **Brighten Dark Photos** - Automatically adjust lighting
3. **Enhance Color Vibrancy** - Improve color saturation and appeal
4. **Upscale Images** - Increase image resolution
5. **Perfect Lighting** - Automatic lighting adjustments
6. **Smart Corrections** - Fix distortion, straighten horizons, correct perspective
7. **Batch Processing** - Enhance multiple photos at once
